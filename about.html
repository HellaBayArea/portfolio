About

Kyle Ramirez is a storyteller in the Bay Area. He directs AI tools until the song or the picture in his head is the one on the screen. He is not a classically trained musician or illustrator. He writes in English and Japanese.

Paper Crane Studio is the home for that work: Akari, the metal videos, the songs, and the art that should outlast a feed.

  • MusicSuno, English and Japanese, metal

  • DirectionMulti-step AI pipelines

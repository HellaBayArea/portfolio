プロフィールAbout Kyle

I'm Kyle, a 38-year-old creator from the Bay Area who fell hard for what AI can do. I'm not a trained musician or illustrator. I'm a storyteller who learned to direct AI tools until the picture or the song in my head showed up on the screen.

I grew up on scary movies like Alien, Resident Evil, Friday the 13th, and The Ring, so a lot of my art turns them into something fun, usually with my tuxedo cat Munchkin in costume. I write songs in English and Japanese, build full music videos from storyboards, and I use my work to give back, like my charity single for children with disabilities.

This site is the permanent home for all of it, because great work shouldn't get lost in a feed.
